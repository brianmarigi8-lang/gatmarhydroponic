Why Hydroponic Fodder

💧

Less Water, More Yield

Produce 36kg of fresh fodder per m² every 7 days using a fraction of the water traditional fodder farming needs.

📈

12x Return on Seed

1kg of sprouted barley seed yields up to 12kg of fresh fodder — a dramatically efficient feed conversion.

🌾

High-Protein Feed

Barley fodder delivers around 15% crude protein, giving your livestock and poultry a nutrient-dense, consistent diet.

🐄

Built for Livestock & Poultry

Units are sized to match your herd or flock, so you produce exactly the fodder volume you need.

♻️

Less Land Required

Produce more feed per square meter than open-field growing, freeing up land for other uses.

🎓

Hands-On Training Included

We don't just install your unit — we train your team on maintenance and day-to-day fodder production.

Fodder Crops We Grow

🌾 Barley

Our top performer — barley fodder delivers around 15% crude protein, making it an excellent high-energy feed for livestock and poultry.

🌾 Wheat

A reliable, widely available option that sprouts quickly and produces consistent, palatable fodder.

🌾 Millet

A hardy grain suited to a variety of climates, ideal for farmers looking to diversify their feed source.
